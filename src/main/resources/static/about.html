About Us

Turning Busy Thoughts into Calm, Clear Action

Who We Are

Karya Planner is your smart daily planning companion designed to bring clarity and calm to busy lives. Whether you are a student juggling assignments, a working professional managing deadlines, or someone trying to stay on top of everyday chores, Karya Planner helps you organize what matters most—without the stress of complicated planners.

Why We Built Karya Planner

Most to-do lists still force you to do all the hard work: sorting tasks, guessing how long things will take, and deciding where to start. When you have too much on your plate, even making a list feels exhausting. We created Karya Planner so you can simply empty your mind in plain, everyday words and let smart assistance turn your thoughts into an organized step-by-step plan.

How Karya Planner Helps You Every Day

  • Effortless Brain Dump: Just type everything on your mind in one place—no need to worry about order or formatting. Karya Planner automatically breaks your thoughts into neat, individual tasks.
  • Smart Priorities & Quick Tips: Each task is automatically ranked from most urgent to least urgent with helpful tips on how to tackle it, so you always know what to do first.
  • Realistic Deadlines You Control: Karya Planner suggests a sensible completion time for every task, and you can easily adjust any deadline or priority whenever your plans change.
  • Interactive Calendar & Daily Focus: Click any date on the calendar to see what is due that day, alongside a complete overview of all your upcoming tasks.
  • Gentle Reminders & Progress Archive: Tasks within one hour of their deadline stand out clearly so nothing slips through the cracks. Once you tick a task off, it moves neatly into your completed archive with the exact date and time you finished it.
  • Comfortable Themes: Switch effortlessly between a soft Light Mode and a soothing Dark Mode to match your workspace and reduce eye strain.

Our Promise to Users

We believe productivity tools should feel simple, encouraging, and clutter-free. Karya Planner is built to save your time, protect your focus, and give you the satisfaction of finishing your day with a clear mind.
