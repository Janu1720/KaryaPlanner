3. What Karya Planner Does

Karya Planner is a web app that helps you organise your work. You type your thoughts in a free-form note, which we call a Brain Dump. The app uses artificial intelligence to turn it into a clear task list. It then suggests how important each task is, when it might be done, and how to approach it. You can also change priorities and deadlines, view your tasks on a calendar, and keep a record of finished tasks.
